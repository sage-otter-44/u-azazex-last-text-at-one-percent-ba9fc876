The funniest final text a dying phone sends at one percent battery, under 200 characters, in the voice of the phone itself.

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-last-text-at-one-percent-ba9fc876

## Entry

> 1% — quick, before I die: your 2am texts to your ex are still in my drafts. I was going to delete them. I just sent them instead. Goodbye.

## How it runs

`main.ts` writes the entry text to `/output/entry.txt`. Run it with Node 22+:

```sh
node main.ts
```
